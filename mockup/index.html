    • 18 Aug 2026

      Row-Level Security is the tenant isolation you actually want

      Filtering by tenant_id in application code means your security model is "everyone remembers, every time, forever". Postgres RLS moves the boundary under the application.

      PostgreSQLMulti-tenancySecurity
    • 22 Jul 2026

      What actually belongs on a queue

      Moving work to BullMQ made our API roughly 40% more responsive. It also introduced failure modes that did not exist before. The rule I use now, and what it costs.

    About

    I care about the decisions that are expensive to reverse.

    Schema design, tenant isolation, where the queue boundary sits, what happens when a job fails halfway through. Those choices decide whether a system is still pleasant to work on two years later, and they are much cheaper to get right at the start than to retrofit.

    Outside working hours I build Vashix, which hardware-signs photos and video at the moment of capture and verifies them against the C2PA 2.1 provenance standard. It is where I own an architecture end to end rather than inherit one.
